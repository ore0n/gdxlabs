Burek and Karlovacko – Skateboard trip Croatia

March 19, 2015
SkateboardingTour
By gabriel92

Enough of skating our local indoor skatepark. We still had to wait for spring, so we packed Miran´s car and left to Split in Croatia. Our next Skateboard trip:

The crew consisted of Miran, who is Croatian, Joel from Australia, Samuel from Germany, Luki the Austrian, me as half Colombian and Ari, Miran´s baby dog.

The perfect gang for a short skate trip to start in the skate season of 2015. Miran´s Croatian language skills made it much easier for us, he organized a really smooth apartment in the center of the city. After checking in, we were so hyped to skate because it had 15-20 degrees whereas in our hometown we had around 0 degrees. We bought some Karlovackos, that´s the name of a Croatian beer, for snacks some Bureks, this is some Croatian food which made us all addicted, we had a cruise through the city center and little session on the promenade next to the ocean and under the palms. Skateboarding and filming was our main focus of the trip, so the next day we headed early to the local spot to warm up and later to hit some of the plenty street spots in Split.

During our warm up session we met Mario and Leo, two locals who were really nice guys, who brought us to many amazing places in Split  and went skating with us every day.

So we could film a lot of stuff, but not without fighting our fears and our legs got sore every day because the spots were big and not easy to skate. Samuel ripped so hard and seemed to know no fear but unfortunately on the third day he had a heel bruise so he wasn´t able to skate for the rest of the trip. At least there are so many beautiful women in Split so he didn´t get bored. One man down and with heavy legs from skating every day the missions went on, so also Luki rolled his ankle and wasn´t able to skate anymore.

From this time we knew we had to keep it mellow, and we concentrated more on the nice weather, the ladies, the ocean, Karlovackos and Bureks. 

All together it was an amazing trip and the time passed too fast, i recommend everybody Split as destination for a skate tour. If you want to see more check out the clip of our trip which was made :
